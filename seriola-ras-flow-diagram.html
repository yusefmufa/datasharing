Sheet 2 — Tank strategy (§2)

One large round tank — not several small ones

This is a fast, schooling, pelagic fish whose courtship is a high-speed chase. Cramped tanks suppress spawning and cause wall-strike injuries. Build the 10 m³ tank now; plan a larger round spawning tank as the fish mature.

Now

Culture / quarantine tank

Capture, holding, domestication. Low broodstock density (3–15 kg/m³).

Round · ~10 m³ · Ø≈3.3 × 1.2 m · dual drain

Phase 4

Spawning tank

Move fish once conditioned, or build this first to skip the migration.

Round · ≥20 m³ (ideally 30–50) · Ø≈5–6 m

Mandatory

Separate quarantine

Hold new wild arrivals several weeks; treat flukes before joining broodstock.

Small offline tank · own filtration

Tank build options (all proven): HDPE/PE · fibreglass (GRP) · or the low-cost galvanized-steel ring + food-grade liner used in northern Chile. Dark interior + ~80% shade cloth. Geometry (round + proper dual drain) matters more than raw volume.

Sheet 5 — Artificial seawater & salt cost (§6)

Salt quantity & running cost

Full-strength seawater ≈ 33–35 kg salt per m³. You pay for the first fill; after that, evaporation costs no salt (you top up with freshwater) and denitrification lets you avoid dumping salt water.

Denitrification reactor + freshwater evaporation top-up = your real salt-recovery strategy. Use bulk salt for holding/grow-out; switch to complete reef-grade salt only for maturation, eggs and larvae — that is not where to economize.

Sheet 6 — General arrangement

Building layout & footprint — plan view

Compact shed ≈ 8.0 × 6.0 m for the 10 m³ phase. Culture tank central; treatment skid along one wall for short pipe runs; plant & make-up in one corner; quarantine offline. Dashed circle = future spawning tank (needs its own larger bay/expansion).

Sheet 7 — Phased roadmap (§11)

Build order

Phase 0

Paper & permits

PRODUCE / IMARPE authorization; finalize geometry & parts list.

Phase 1

Build & cycle

Tank + loop on RO-made artificial seawater; cycle biofilter 4–8 weeks empty.

Phase 2

Capture & quarantine

Wild fish in small batches; deworm, get them eating.

Phase 3

Domestication

Low density, wean to pellets, stabilize over one season.

Phase 4

Photothermal spawning

Drive temp + photoperiod; collect floating eggs.

Phase 5

Scale / larvae

Larval rearing — the recognized bottleneck; a whole second project.
