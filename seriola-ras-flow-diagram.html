Sheet 1 — Process flow diagram

Water treatment loop & equipment flow

Water recirculates clockwise 1 → 8. Culture water leaves the tanks, is cleaned of solids, ammonia and CO₂, re-oxygenated and disinfected, then returned. Utilities (air, oxygen, heat) inject into the loop; solids and foam waste leave it.

Engineering notes

What each system has to do

Water & pipework

  • Gravity down, pump up: tanks → settler → sump run on gravity; pumps then lift water through biofilter, degasser, oxygenator and UV.
  • Marine-grade only: PVC/HDPE pipe, titanium heat exchanger, FRP or lined tanks — salt water eats mild steel.
  • Slope solids lines ≥1% so settled sludge keeps moving to the settler.

Air, oxygen & heat

  • Blower feeds MBBR mixing and the skimmer venturi — one machine, two jobs.
  • Pure O₂ (concentrator or LOX) into the oxygenator is the real safety margin at high density.
  • Heat pump + titanium HX holds 23 °C; insulate the shed to cut running cost.

Waste & safety

  • Two waste exits: settled sludge from the settler, foam/DOC from the skimmer — both leave the loop, neither returns.
  • Alarms + standby: low-DO and pump-fail alarms, standby pump, and a backup generator prevent a total loss.
  • Biosecurity: UV on the return line; quarantine new stock before adding.
